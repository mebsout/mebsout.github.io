About me

I have been a co-founder of Functori since 2021.

I was previously an engineer at OCamlPro, and before that a postdoctoral research scholar at the University of Iowa where I worked on the model checker Kind 2 with Cesare Tinelli. I did my PhD at the LRI in Université Paris-Sud under the direction of Sylvain Conchon and Fatiha Zaïdi.

My area of research is formal methods, and my interests lie in model checking and satisfiability modulo theories (SMT).
